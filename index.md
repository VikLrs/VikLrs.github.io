## Introduction

<img src="assets/images/me.jpg" alt="Photo" hspace="20" width="30%" align="right"/> I am a master's student in Phonetics at the University of Helsinki. My academic interests include phonology, historical linguistics and language variation. I also hold a master's degree in Classical Philology, with a focus on Latin and Ancient Greek.

I am especially interested in how languages change over time and how phonetic and phonological variation can be studied using computational methods.
